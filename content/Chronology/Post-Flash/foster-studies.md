# The Foster Studies
The Norma Foster Studies, led by the named [[gcsa|GCSA-funded]] researcher, were an archive of her led experiments and research on the origins, precautions, and intricacies of the [[rad|RAD]] phenomenon, aswell as the nature of each strand in proximity to each other. The data collected from these studies would directly contribute to both the education of RAD throughout [[pfa|PFA]] and the eventual [[rad-vaccines|RAD-Vaccine]].
