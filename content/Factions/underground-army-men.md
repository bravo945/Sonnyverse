# The Underground Army Men

![[army-men]]

The Chicago Underground's Army Men are a unique hysterical phenomenon involving sparse crowds of strangers marching in formation through the Chicago metro. In such crowds, people are often seen holding various objects resembling rifles laid upon a soldier's arms, from sticks and scraps to actual guns and crafted weaponry. The phenomenon is sparsely detailed, with no memory of its origin, though sightings go back as far as 2045.
# Hysterical Protocol
The Army Men rarely act out of sync, marching in rigid, square form. Commonly front to the pack is a designated commander, who commands the crowd through yelling and hand signals. Marching goes on indefinitely, a devoted army-men never stopping their march until collapse, serious exhaustion, and/or death. Deserting members either are forgotten about or whispered among the crowd as "traitors" or "cowards", while those who collapse in march are saluted briefly before being left behind, even if one is seen still breathing.

If a fleet reaches a dead end, they will simply start marching the other way. Any questions or attempts to converse with crowds end up ignored. If threatened, the crowd does not acknowledge the attack until prompted by their commander, in which all order breaks into a chaotic attack on such threat, regardless of how outmatched either force may be. No matter how many survivors are left, the group always reforms immediately after the threat is neutralized, marching again in their now wounded state.
# Faction Responses
Most groups have learned to let the marchers move along, even if it means opening the gates of their bases. However, in response to the [[the-collapse|Collapse]], some groups will scan crowds for genuine weaponry, discarding of such. Other factions consider the hysteria as a threat, attacking the men on-sight. In these cases are army-men unlikely to march around certain stretches of subway through maintenance or sewer tunnels in order to avoid hostile groups.
## Uncaring Factions
- [[sticks-and-stones-union|Sticks and Stones Union]]
	- Hobos often direct army-traffic, their signals and faction members often being the only stimuli a crowd will acknowledge. Crowds will sometimes follow the marks of the union.
- [[white-star|White-Star Groups]]
	- Though dependent on the town, the White Star Militia generally sees it as a waste of time to bother army-men.
## Cautious Factions
- [[fulton-mall|Fulton Mall Scrap Town]]
	- Since the Collapse, the Fulton Mall strips Army-Men of crafted and manufactured weaponry.
## Enemy Factions
- [[unamerica|Unamericans]]
	- Since the Collapse, Unamericans are the only faction considered hostile to the army-men themselves, their crowd engaging the faction on sight, though congregations of Unamericans in open tunnels are already rare from the Collapse.
# The Last March
Toward the end of nuclear winter, the army men lost the hysterical appeal and population of a once dying people forced to rot in the underground. Eventually, this resulted in a final fleet of 10-12 overtly devoted Army-Men. Over a documented week, these men finally dropped to a humble 2, one member eventually succumbing to the floor from a bad fall. The last man, who hadn't looked behind him in about a day or so, saw his lonely stature, upon which he paused, saluted, and stood frozen for "a good ten hours", until collapsing himself. His remains became a monument and memorial for the army-men as a whole, the underground seeing his collapse as the official end to the corridor era.
