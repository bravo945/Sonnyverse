# Radiation Accelerated Disease - 'X-Strand'
# Overview
The X-Strand of the [[rad|Radiation Accelerated Disease]] is a complicated inter-organic system fueled by excessive radioactive material. Though infectious all the same, RAD-X differs from its original RAD counterpart in its connectivity with both other infected and the "biomass" product that the infected depend on. Without a "hivemind" connection to a center of biomass, RAD-X mutants will "devolve" into base RAD
# Transmission
Individuals can only contract RAD-X if they are exposed to mutants or biomass of the X-Strand. Because of its centralized, "hive" culture, RAD-X is dependent on its subjects for disease, and does not travel further beyond them.

# Biomass
Biomass are masses of organic, fungal-like tissue, fueled by warmth, radiation, and decomposed organisms. Biomass structures can grow indefinitely depending on their biotic factors and general intake.

In pursuit of absolute symbiosis with its infected agents, RAD-X biomass supports mutants in all kinds of ways; from sheltering, healing, accelerating mutation, to connecting all mutants under a structure to a general hivemind. The biomass serves as more than a structure, being a local network for the disease to consciously impose and improve itself over the general network.



# Relations with Other Strains

## RAD
- Neutral
RAD instances are recognized as fellow mutants, though their disconnection from any hivemind severs them of any deeper recurring alliance. 
## RAD-E
- Mixed
	- Not Recognized
In early stages of RAD-X, infected that still retain sentience retain a neutral attitude to RAD-E, only responding appropriately to hostility. Later stages, however, see completely see RAD-E infected as prey, often preferring to target smaller groups.

RAD-X biomass is predatory to RAD-E moss, consuming the matter for radioactive energy.

## RAD-S
- Hostile
RAD-S instances, especially later stages, are mutually hostile to RAD-X, both considering each other as threats.

Cultures of RAD-S and 'X' biomass attempt to conquer one-another in growth.
