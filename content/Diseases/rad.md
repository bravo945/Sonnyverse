# Radiation Accelerated Disease

Radiation Accelerated Disease (RAD) is an abiotic infection originating from anomalous phenomena related to nuclear technology of the [[pre-flash|Pre-Flash]] era. Those apt of infection (Humans, large mammals, etc) are prone to hair loss, dementia, agitation, and a shift in pigmentation. Once infection occurs, an individual will become attracted to radiation, which reinforces and preserves their disease.

# Transmission
RAD sustains itself off of radiated substance, meaning that exposure of RAD comes with exposure to nuclear-radiation. This includes any surface or substance already carrying nuclear-radiation, contaminated food-product, water, locations, and persons. Contact with infected, especially from attacks and other close-contact situations.

Symptoms can arise in as little as a single day to as much as an entire week, depending on where the subject was exposed and how much radiation they were exposed to. Too much radiation during transmission, however, can overwhelm the body before RAD is able to take over, becoming lethal. 
# Mutations
Because of the nature of RAD and its intense exposure to radiation, countless possible mutations can occur on an individual, from excess growths and tumors to even extra limbs and facial features.
