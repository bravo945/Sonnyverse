# Nondescript Generative Substance

Of many names, from the [[gcsa|GCSA]]'s "Nondescript Generative Substance (NGS)" to the colloquial "tumor", the [[rad|RAD]]-based biomatter is a mysterious growth resembling abiotic and biotic qualities. To date, NGA is the only RAD infection that is neither airborne nor predatory on animals and their psychology, at worst a parasitic growth treatable with traditional common disinfectants.
# Origins
NGS does not have a clear origin beyond its relation to RAD. The only clue of its origin resides in its tendency to grow around destroyed fusion power sources, though exceptions are abundant.

the NGS strand seems to have mutated shortly before or after the time RAD-X, in which the trait of solid biomass production became priority over the perversion of hosts, though such a timeline is complete speculation.  
# Diagnostics
Microscopic research by the [[foster-studies|Foster Studies]] indicate colonies of what resemble cellular life, though metabolic organelles are not present, replaced by what are assumed to be structures capable of synthesizing nuclear radiation through unclear means. In result, cells become capable of expanding to near-macro levels before dissolving under their own weight, mysteriously giving way to new cells from their remains. Through this format, its speed enormous compared to available radiation, colonies are able to engulf entire regions of irradiated zones for the entire lifespan of exposure.
